# Riftworld 0.40.4 — Elisar's Aurite and Riftcrystal arsenal

Minecraft Forge 1.20.1 · Java 17

## What's new
- Aurite coin-metal armor, sword, pickaxe, axe and shovel, with medium defensive stats.
- Rare Riftcrystal armor and tools/weapons with stronger defensive and durability stats.
- Rare Riftcrystal ore generation restricted to three Elisar regions: Crystal Rifts, Morok Hollow and Mist Keep.
- Three villager professions for the residential cities: Ducatier, City Guard and Ranger, each with a unique job site and trades.
- New pixel-art item, ore, workstation and vanilla-player armor textures; crafting, smelting, blasting, repair ingredients and localized names.

## Install
Put `riftworld-0.40.4.jar` in your Minecraft `mods` folder. The `riftworld-dt-0.40.4.jar` is optional and only for users who already install Dynamic Trees. Back up worlds before updating.

The release also includes `elisar_gear_catalog.png`, a resource preview (not an in-game screenshot).
