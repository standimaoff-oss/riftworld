# Riftworld v0.49.0 — AI concept references

Three selected AI-generated concept sheets that anchor the visual direction of this
release. Each sheet was generated as a candidate pair and the **final selection below
is the one that shipped** — no further variants are pending.

| File | Subject | Selection | Shipped direction |
|------|---------|-----------|-------------------|
| `01_elisar_sky_skiff_airship.png` | Elisar sky-skiff airship | **option 2 of 2** | A sail-rigged skiff gliding above the cloud sea over the purple Elisar ocean; the selected frame has the cleaner silhouette, the readable canvas sail and the warmer rim light that match the in-game ocean palette. |
| `02_riftcrystal_colossus_guardian.png` | Riftcrystal Colossus Guardian | **option 1 of 2** | A towering crystal-armoured colossus carrying a halberd under an aurora sky; the selected frame keeps the faceted cyan/magenta Riftcrystal plate, the readable silhouette and the tall shoulder horns that the 3D chestplate and helmet models reproduce. |
| `03_astral_forge_sanctum.png` | Astral Forge Sanctum | **option 2 of 2** | A star-domed forge sanctum centred on a rune-lit anvil; the selected frame has the stronger dome geometry, the clear rune anvil focus and the deep indigo/gold contrast used for the Astral Forge palette. |

## How these were used

- **Sky-skiff** — the Elisar settlement and the sky-lift fiction; it informs the
  silhouette language of the airship structures and the warm sunset rim light.
- **Colossus Guardian** — the direct visual brief for the **Riftcrystal Strong Armor**
  worn model (`02_riftcrystal_strong_armor.png`): faceted dark slate-iron plate, swept
  crystal horns, cyan T-visor and magenta/cyan ribcage chevrons.
- **Astral Forge Sanctum** — the forge-palette brief for the **Aurite Medium Armor**
  gold trim and the Riftcrystal emissive glow that is rendered through the
  `RenderType.eyes` glow atlas.
